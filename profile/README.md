# 🏛️ The Potted Historian

Welcome to the central GitHub Organization for **The Potted Historian** research suite, historical archival projects, and botanical software ecosystem.

We specialize in translating centuries of botanical chaos into structured digital systems. This organization houses primary research monographs analyzing pre-17th-century herbal literature, historical orchid studies (*Orchis mascula* / *Satyrion*), and custom specimen tracking software. We build custom Python desktop applications, SQLite schemas, and digital twin sensor integrations to track rare orchids, digitize historical herbal manuscripts, and bridge the gap between 16th-century woodcuts and modern microclimate telemetry. 

It is the only engineering environment where our databases require regular schema maintenance, our archival research requires cross-referencing Renaissance Latin, and our hardware tests require checking if a tropical orchid is currently thriving or just plotting its next dramatic decline.
